Out of scope as a topology decision. Where-things-run is unchanged: the single Next.js deployable, in-process pg-boss workers, and managed Postgres (ADR-0001, ADR-0004) all stay as they are. This rework removes the `draft` worker/queue and adds no new container, process, or external system, so the deployment topology is untouched.

One operational note carries into the code phase (not a topology change): removing the `draft` worker leaves its pg-boss queue row and any in-flight `draft` jobs orphaned in Postgres - pg-boss does not drop a queue when a worker stops being registered. Slice 1 must run a one-time `boss.deleteQueue("draft")` after the draft worker registration is removed, and Slice 1/3 likewise `boss.deleteQueue("qualify-prospect")` once that worker is retired (both documented runbook steps, not Drizzle migrations); in pg-boss 12 each call deletes the queue and any remaining jobs in one statement, so no separate drain is needed and the jobs monitor does not show a frozen backlog. The `queued`-status drafts abandoned by the draft removal are intentional (the drafting stage is gone).
